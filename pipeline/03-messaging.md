## The core message

**"Elegí un taller. La primera clase es gratis."**

Pick a course. The first class is free.

That is the headline on the page and it is the whole campaign. Here is why it beats every alternative I tried.

Valentina found two free competitors and one that charges around $130.000 a year. In that market our price is not the argument, and we do not publish it anyway. What we have that nobody else has is the ability to say: come and sit in the room first, and pay nothing to do it. That answers the real objection, which is not "how much" but "will I feel out of place".

It is also the only sentence on the page that names an action a person can take this week.

## Proof points, in the order they appear

1. 47 offerings, on a real timetable, with the teacher named.
2. In person in Argüello, or by video call, or both in the same room.
3. A certificate from Universidad Blas Pascal at 80 per cent attendance.
4. Classes from 2 March to 30 November, and you can join in any month.
5. A person answers the phone.

Each one is checkable against the sheet or the programme document. I have written nothing I cannot source.

## What I changed on the page, and why

| Was going to say | Says instead | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Programa para adultos mayores de 50 años" | "Elegí un taller. La primera clase es gratis." | Never lead with their age. Age is how you target, not how you speak. The audience knows how old it is |
| "Formación integral y crecimiento personal" | "Idiomas, arte, filosofía, tecnología, bridge y golf" | Institutional language describes the institution. Nouns describe what you will actually do on Wednesday |
| "Consultar aranceles" | "El arancel se paga por mes... preguntanos y te lo decimos en el momento, sin compromiso" | Dodging the price question reads as hiding something. Naming the mechanism and offering the answer reads as straight |
| "Inscribite ahora" | "Quiero probar una clase" | The commitment we ask for should match the commitment they are ready to make |
| "Contactanos" | "WhatsApp 351 3 261002" as a button | An abstraction became an action. This audience already uses WhatsApp: it is how their classes are run |

## The two-audience split

Valentina flagged, unverified, that a share of enquiries in this category come from the learner's adult children. I have written for both from one offer.

**To the learner**, on the page: second person, concrete, no age reference. "Podés probar cualquier taller sin pagar nada antes de decidir."

**To the adult child**, for social posts: the reassurance they are actually looking for, which is that their parent will not be alone or embarrassed. "Un grupo chico, el mismo día cada semana, y la primera clase no se paga." Same offer. Different worry answered.

I have not built this into the page, because the page has one job. It belongs in the launch posts.

## Launch kit, first two weeks

Two channels only: Instagram (@aulauniversitaria2020) and Facebook. Both are already owned and already have the audience. No paid acquisition. In a programme billed monthly, referral and retention are where the growth is, and a peso spent on ads is a peso not collected in fees.

**Ignore:** Google Ads, TikTok, LinkedIn, a newsletter tool. None of them reach a Córdoba over-50 audience better than a teacher telling their group to bring a friend.

## Ask the teachers for one thing

Twenty seven people with their own reputations are worth more than any brand line. I need 80 words from each, in their own voice, on why they teach what they teach. That is nine hours of someone's time across the year and it is the highest return item on this list.

## What I refused to write

- **A price, or a range, or "desde".** Nothing in any source states a fee. Inventing one to look competitive against PUAM's $15.000 would be a lie that a phone call exposes in a week.
- **A testimonial.** We have none on record. I will write real ones the day the coordination team collects them with consent.
- **"Mantené tu mente activa" and every variant.** It is fear copy aimed at people worried about decline. It tests well and it is beneath this organisation.
- **"Plazas limitadas".** Some of these workshops need students. Manufacturing scarcity on a class that is not filling is both dishonest and, on a 10-student minimum, actively counterproductive.
- **A claim that we are the largest, the best or the only.** We are one of four. What we can say truthfully is that we are the only one that lets you try first, and that is enough.

## The honest cost of being honest

Not publishing a fee will lose us some enquiries to UPAMI and the Universidad Provincial, both free. I am telling you that plainly rather than writing copy that pretends the choice is not there. The trial class is the answer to it: someone who has already sat in the room and liked it will ask what it costs, and by then the question is different.

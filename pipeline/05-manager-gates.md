**From:** Delia Fontán, Manager
**Covering:** the full pipeline, 6 August 2026

## Decision

**Proceed to publish.** One page, built on the live catalogue, with the free trial class as the way in. It replaces the 2021 page, which comes down the same day.

Reasoning: Valentina found that no competitor in Córdoba publishes a timetable, and that we are the only one offering a trial class. Those two facts together are a position nobody else can copy this year, and we can occupy it with a page that costs nothing to host. The work is small enough that the organisation can carry it, which is the test I apply to everything.

Cost: no monthly hosting cost. One person, roughly two hours a month, described below.

## What I stopped

**Online enrolment and payment.** Tomás offered it as concept C. I refused it before design work started.

The organisation collects fees monthly, in a window between the 1st and the 15th, and has no engineer. A booking and payment system needs an owner every week, a reconciliation process, a refund policy and a data controller. Nobody has named that person. A plan that adds work without naming who does it is not a plan.

Revisit when someone is named and has the hours.

## Regulatory and ethical position

Not legal advice. These are the duties I can see, and a lawyer should confirm them.

**GDPR.** The page collects nothing. No form, no account, no cookie, no analytics, no tracking script, no third-party embed. There is no personal data processing to have a lawful basis for, which is the cleanest position available and one I would like to keep. The moment anyone adds analytics, that changes and needs consent under Article 6 with a real basis, not a banner that assumes it. Argentina's Ley 25.326 applies locally and the same reasoning holds.

**EU AI Act.** No AI system is deployed to the public here. The five agents built this site: they do not run inside it, and no visitor talks to a model. **Article 50** transparency duties on informing people they are interacting with an AI system therefore do not bite, because nobody is. If a chatbot is ever added, Article 50 applies from that day and the disclosure goes in the first message. **Annex III** high-risk categories include access to education and vocational training. A page that displays a timetable does not decide who is admitted, so it sits outside that. An automated enrolment or selection system would need this assessed properly before it ships.

**Trust, which is the part that actually matters here.** The audience is a group that regulators watch closely for a reason: they are targeted disproportionately by pressure selling. We have therefore published a page with no countdown, no false scarcity, no fear copy about memory decline, no invented testimonial and no invented price. When we do not know something, the page says so and gives a phone number. That costs us some enquiries. It is the correct trade for an organisation whose students stay for years.

One thing to watch: the page publishes 27 teachers' names, which are already public in the programme document. Anyone who wants their name removed should be able to ask and have it happen. That is a coordination process, and it does not exist yet.

## Executive summary

The programme was in good health and almost invisible. Its public course page had been advertising the second semester of 2021 for five years, naming two teachers who no longer work here.

Four organisations in Córdoba serve this audience. Two are free. None publishes a timetable. Only we offer a free trial class, and that offer was buried in a document almost nobody reads.

We have published one page that reads the coordination team's own spreadsheet live, shows all 47 offerings with day, time, teacher and format, and puts the free trial class in the first sentence. It costs nothing to run, needs no new system from the coordination team, and reads at 20px with 9:1 contrast because the audience is over 50 and that is simply what the job required.

What I want next, in order: name the two owners in the table above, add one Spanish column to the sheet, and watch the phone. If enquiries rise and nobody answers, we will have built a very accessible way to be disappointed.

## What I was asked

Find what the competition does, and find what is worth fixing about our own web presence. I have not proposed a solution. Three problems follow, sized and evidenced. Tomás decides what to build.

## 1. The competitive picture

Four organisations in Córdoba serve people over 50. I read all four public sites.

| Provider | Price published | Days and times published | Teachers named | Filter or search | Enrol by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PUAM** (UNC, Facultad de Ciencias Médicas) | Yes. $15.000 per month, plus $10.000 annual registration, instalments 1 to 4 confirmed and 5 to 8 to be confirmed | No | No | No | WhatsApp 351 8040199 |
| **Comenzar** (Universidad Católica de Córdoba) | No | No | No | No | Phone, WhatsApp or email, weekdays 10 to 12 and 14:30 to 18:30 |
| **UPAMI** (UNC with PAMI) | Free for PAMI members | No | No | No | pami.org.ar form |
| **Universidad Provincial de Córdoba** | Free, over 55s | No | No | No | Provincial form |
| **aulauniversitaria** (us) | No | No, the 2021 page is still live | Two names, both wrong for 2026 | No | Phone |

### Three findings from that table

**Finding 1. Two competitors are free, and we charge.**
UPAMI and the Universidad Provincial both cost nothing. PUAM charges roughly $130.000 for a year once registration and eight instalments are counted. We sit somewhere in that range and publish nothing. A person comparing options sees two free choices, one priced choice, and one that will not say. Unverified: I cannot state where our fee sits, because no figure exists in any source I can reach.

**Finding 2. Nobody in this market publishes a timetable.**
Not one of the four shows a prospective student which day and hour a course runs before they make contact. PUAM lists dozens of courses with a price and no schedule. This is the single largest gap in the category, and it is the one thing we already have: 47 offerings with day, time, teacher, format and period, sitting in a spreadsheet the coordination team already maintains.

**Finding 3. We are the only one offering a free trial class.**
None of the other three offers a way to try a course before paying. Our programme document gives one free class in any workshop to anyone who enrols. It appears in a paragraph on page 2 of a document almost no prospective student will ever open.

## 2. What is wrong with our own presence

Audited against the 2026 programme document and the live sheet.

| What a visitor sees today | What is actually true |
|---|---|
| A courses page headed "Oferta Académica 2º Cuatrimestre 2021" | The current offering is the first semester of 2026 |
| Julia Liliana Rizzi teaching visual arts, Pablo Ravera teaching Italian | Neither teaches on the 2026 programme. Painting is Cecilia Macedo Rivera. Italian is Carla Palmaghini, Gabriela Rodríguez Junco and Giuliano Malinverno |
| "Classes are virtual until in-person workshops resume" | 40 of 47 offerings are in person. The campus runs hybrid classrooms |
| No course list, no schedule, no teacher roster on the pages meant to carry them | 47 offerings, 27 teachers, full schedule, all current |
| No Instagram link on the programme page | The account @aulauniversitaria2020 is active |

The programme is in good health. The public record of it is five years old.

---

## 3. The three problems worth solving, in order

**Problem A. The catalogue is invisible at the moment of choice.**
Size: every prospective student, every year. A person who wants to know whether we teach Italian on a Thursday cannot find out without phoning during office hours. Evidence: our own public pages, and the fact that the current catalogue exists only in a Word document and a spreadsheet.

**Problem B. The strongest asset in the category is buried.**
The free trial class is our only structural advantage over two free competitors and one cheaper one. Nobody encountering us online learns it exists. Evidence: the offer appears once, in the methodology section of the programme document.

**Problem C. The published record contradicts the real programme.**
A visitor who finds the site meets two teachers who left and a semester that ended five years ago. Evidence: the table in section 2. This is a trust problem before it is a marketing problem.

## 4. What I could not establish

- **The fee.** No figure exists in the programme document or on any page of the organisation's site. I will not estimate one. Anyone who needs it should call 3513 261002 or write to info@promover.org.ar.
- **Enrolment and retention numbers.** No records were made available. I cannot say whether enrolment is rising or falling, and I have not implied either.
- **Where enquiries come from today.** No source. My working assumption that a share of enquiries come from adult children is a pattern from the wider category, not a measurement of this organisation. Marked unverified. It would be settled by asking the next twenty callers one question.

## 5. Two facts the build will have to handle

Both are real and both are in the live data.

1. **A timetable clash.** Three courses sit at Thursday 15:00 (Emotional Management and Mindfulness, Train Your Mind, Italian I). Two are in person on the same campus. A day view will show this to the public. I recommend showing it rather than hiding it, and I have flagged it to the coordination team as a scheduling question, not a display question.
2. **Key-person concentration.** Carla Palmaghini teaches seven of the nine Italian offerings. Luciano Camio leads four. This is not a website problem. It is in the brief because Delia asked for risks that any growth plan has to survive.
